[Adblock Plus 2.0]
! Title: Search Engine Ads - Startpage and DuckDuckGo
! Description: Block Startpage ad frames and hide search ads on both engines.
! Version: 2026.09.13.3
! Scope: startpage.com and duckduckgo.com, including their subdomains.
! Startpage rules checked against user-provided Chrome page HTML.
! DuckDuckGo rules adapted from EasyList; live browser blocking is not verified.
! Vivaldi: Enable Block Trackers and Ads, then open Manage Sources.
! Under Ad Blocking Sources, use Add List to import this file.
! Disable "Allow ads from our partners" and reload the search page.
!
! --- Startpage ---
! Target: https://www.startpage.com/do/search?q=parallels+pricing&segment=startpage.vivaldi
!
! Block the ad iframe requests, including /ads?... and /ads/i/iframe.html.
||startpage.com/sp/afs/ads^$subdocument,domain=startpage.com
!
! Collapse the top and bottom ad containers and their reserved space.
startpage.com###gcsa-top
startpage.com###gcsa-bottom
!
! Fallback for ad frames moved outside those containers; language-independent.
startpage.com##iframe[src^="https://www.startpage.com/sp/afs/ads"]
!
! Fallback inside the Startpage-hosted ad iframe, confirmed in its copied HTML.
startpage.com###adBlock[role="region"]
!
! --- DuckDuckGo ---
! Source: EasyList, retrieved 2026-09-13; domain lists narrowed to duckduckgo.com.
! https://github.com/easylist/easylist/blob/master/easylist/easylist_specific_hide.txt
!
! Search ads and sponsored results, including sidebar ads and older layouts.
duckduckgo.com###ads
duckduckgo.com##.highlight_sponsored
duckduckgo.com##.result--ad > .result__body
duckduckgo.com##.sponsored
duckduckgo.com##[data-layout$="_ads"]
duckduckgo.com##[data-layout="ad"]
duckduckgo.com##.js-results-ads
duckduckgo.com##.js-sidebar-ads > .nrn-react-div
duckduckgo.com##.results--ads
!
! Sections explicitly marked as ads, including sponsored product sections.
duckduckgo.com##.vertical-section-divider:has(span.badge--ad-wrap)
duckduckgo.com##section > div[class]:has(button[data-testid="ad-badge-ad-tooltip-products"])
